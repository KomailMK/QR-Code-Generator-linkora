Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers on how Linkora QR codes work, how they're stored, and how to get the best scan reliability.

Do Linkora QR codes ever expire?

Never. We encode your destination URL directly into the QR pattern rather than routing through a paid redirect proxy. As long as your destination link is live, the QR code will work forever.

Do I need an account to create and download a QR card?

No account is required to design and download high-resolution PNG cards. Signing in is completely optional and lets you save designs to your library and sync your brand defaults.

What color contrast works best for phone cameras?

Keep the QR code ink noticeably darker than the QR background paper. Our built-in platform presets and themes are pre-tested for instant recognition on iOS and Android cameras.

Can I re-edit or re-download my saved QR codes later?

Yes. When signed in, open "My QR codes" in the top bar to load any saved card back into the studio or download its full branded PNG card with one click.
